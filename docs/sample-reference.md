# Updated format and sample review

User supplied UWA model paper 2024.pdf as a sample and requested 40 MCQs in one hour. The sample cover and first question page were rendered and inspected: Paper 1 states 40 questions, a one-hour duration and three answer choices, and includes illustrated reasoning questions. Text extraction uses legacy Tamil font encoding, so visual inspection was necessary.

This project follows that format with 40 original MCQs per paper, 60 minutes, three options by default, and 2.5 marks each to retain a total of 100. Imports also accept four-option questions. Fifteen new SVG reasoning illustrations (patterns, clocks and grids) are included across the five papers. These are original practice questions, not transcriptions of the sample, and are not claimed to match its difficulty or the current official curriculum until teacher review. The sample PDF, provincial crest and official branding are not redistributed in the source bundle.

Diagram keys refer only to bundled assets. Accessible text descriptions accompany each image. Paid answer keys remain server-side and outside the static frontend. Static illustrations reveal only the information needed to answer the question.
